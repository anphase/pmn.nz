I've just finished reading James Clear's book, [Atomic Habits][1], and I found it quite [brilliant][2].
One of its biggest lessons is the power of small changes and how they compound over time. I'm paraphrasing here:
> You get what you repeat. Time will magnify it.

I love this idea. It's one more thing I'm going to be deliberate about in my workflow.
